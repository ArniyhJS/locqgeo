# LocqGeo Product Thesis

**Status:** Working hypothesis
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## The Product We Should Prove First

LocqGeo should first be sold as a **B2B field-verification control plane for Nigerian organizations that manage distributed workers, agents, or inspection sites**.

The initial promise is deliberately narrow:

> Help operations teams collect location-aware visit evidence, identify exceptions, and produce an audit-supporting record without requiring new field hardware.

LocqGeo does not prove that a person performed every task at a site. A successful check-in is evidence that an authorized device submitted a claim near an assigned location at a particular time, subject to the configured signals and the limits described in the security documentation.

## Initial Customer Profile

The first customer should have:

- 20–200 field workers, agents, technicians, or inspectors;
- recurring site visits that are currently reported through paper, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, or screenshots;
- a supervisor who spends meaningful time chasing exceptions;
- a measurable cost from missed visits, unverifiable claims, or slow reporting;
- an operations decision-maker who can approve a short pilot.

The first buyer is likely a Head of Field Operations, Agent Network Operations, Compliance, Risk, or Service Delivery. The economic buyer and daily user may be different people.

## Abuja-First Wedge

Abuja is a practical starting market because it concentrates development contractors, public-sector suppliers, distributed infrastructure operators, and regional operations teams. This is a sales and access advantage, not proof of market demand.

Prioritize the segment where the team has the strongest warm access:

1. solar and distributed field service;
2. facilities and private security;
3. development contractors and NGOs;
4. agent banking and POS networks;
5. agrifinance and agricultural inspections.

The ordering is a sales hypothesis. It must be revised using interviews, pilot conversion, deployment friction, and measurable customer value.

## What Is In Scope First

- tenant and team administration;
- worker/device enrollment and site assignments;
- QR plus location-aware check-in;
- online and offline capture with explicit pending status;
- supervisor exception review;
- searchable verification history;
- CSV export and API access;
- basic audit log and role-based access.

## Deferred Until Evidence Exists

- consumer rewards, wallets, and cash withdrawals;
- public campaign discovery and leaderboards;
- pan-African payment integrations;
- NFC, BLE, Wi-Fi, and ultrasonic verification;
- access control and credential issuance;
- claims of universal proof, tamper-proof records, or guaranteed fraud prevention;
- broad expansion across the listed vertical portfolio.

## Falsifiable Thesis

At least three Abuja-based organizations will pay for a 2–4 week pilot if LocqGeo can demonstrate that it reduces unverifiable field visits or reporting effort without materially increasing worker friction.

The thesis is not validated by documentation, a demo, or free-user signups. It is validated by paid pilots, observed usage, measurable operational improvement, and renewal or expansion.
