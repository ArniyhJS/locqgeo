# Initial ICP and Buyer

**Status:** Working hypothesis
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Ideal Customer Profile

LocqGeo should initially target Nigerian organizations with distributed operations, repeated physical visits, and weak evidence trails. The best early customer is not necessarily the largest organization; it is one with an urgent operational owner and a short path to a pilot.

### Strong Signals

- field activity is coordinated through spreadsheets, WhatsApp, paper forms, or manual calls;
- supervisors cannot reliably distinguish completed, delayed, duplicated, and disputed visits;
- transport or service allowances depend on self-reported activity;
- the organization already has Android phones in the field;
- the customer can nominate a small pilot region and one accountable supervisor;
- the customer can provide baseline data for comparison.

### Disqualifying Signals for an Early Pilot

- the customer requires formal regulatory acceptance before testing;
- the customer has no named operational owner;
- the workflow requires biometric identity, guaranteed indoor positioning, or continuous tracking;
- the customer expects LocqGeo to replace an entire workforce-management suite immediately;
- the customer will not share a baseline or define a success measure.

## Buyer and User Map

| Role             | Concern                                       | Evidence of value                                   |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Economic buyer   | Cost leakage, service quality, audit exposure | Lower disputed claims or operating cost             |
| Operations lead  | Visibility and exception handling             | Faster review and fewer unresolved visits           |
| Field supervisor | Easy assignment and escalation workflow       | Less manual follow-up                               |
| Field worker     | Low-friction check-in and fair treatment      | Fast capture, clear pending status, appeal path     |
| IT/security      | Access, privacy, integration, reliability     | Audit logs, scoped credentials, documented controls |

## Discovery Questions

1. What field activity must be proven, and why?
2. How is it recorded today?
3. What percentage is disputed, late, or unverifiable?
4. What does one unverifiable visit cost the organization?
5. Who reviews exceptions and how long does that take?
6. What happens when there is no network or GPS accuracy is poor?
7. What evidence would the customer accept, and what would it not accept?
8. Which systems must receive the result?
9. What privacy, labor, donor, or regulatory rules apply?
10. What outcome would justify paying for a pilot and renewing it?

## Commercial Positioning

Lead with the operational outcome:

> Verify assigned field visits, surface exceptions, and export an audit-supporting record from the phones and sites you already have.

Avoid leading with “AI,” “trustless,” “tamper-proof,” “universal,” or a list of future integrations. Those terms create proof burdens before the buyer understands the workflow value.
