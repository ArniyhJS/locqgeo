# Events, Conferences & Concerts

### Problem

Ticketing fraud, proxy attendance, and “ghost” delegates are common. Organizers also lack data on which sessions or zones attendees actually visited.

**Additional challenges:**

- Ticket scalping and duplicate entry cost venues revenue and create safety issues.
- Speakers and sponsors lack data on actual session attendance or booth engagement.
- Delegation certificates for continuing education are often issued on trust.
- Large crowds cause slow manual check-in queues.
- VIP lounges and restricted areas are hard to police without wristband infrastructure.

### How SpottGeo helps

- Venue entrance, session rooms, and sponsor booths become geofences.
- Attendees scan QR codes for check-in, session credits, or badges.
- Organizers get verified attendance, heatmaps, and engagement analytics.

### Key features used

- QR check-in, session-level geofences, visitor ordering, digital badges, leaderboard for engagement.

### Revenue model

- Per-ticket verification fee.
- Event SaaS subscription.
- Analytics add-on for sponsors.

### Example

A tech conference uses SpottVerify to confirm attendees entered each session. CPE credits are issued only after verified attendance, and sponsors see booth visit numbers.

**Additional examples:**

- A music festival issues QR wristbands and verifies entry at main stage and VIP zones.
- A professional conference gives accredited CPE credits only after verified session attendance.
- An exhibition hall ranks exhibitors by verified booth visits and shares heatmaps with sponsors.
- A university open-day verifies which sessions prospective students attended for follow-up recruitment.
