# Healthcare & Pharmaceutical Field Teams

### Problem
Pharma companies send medical reps to clinics but struggle to verify visits. Home-care providers also need proof of service delivery.

**Additional challenges:**
- Medical reps may submit visit reports without actually meeting healthcare professionals.
- Home-care providers need proof of service delivery for insurance reimbursement.
- Clinical trial coordinators struggle to verify patient visit adherence.
- Sampling and promotional inventory are hard to reconcile with actual visits.
- Compliance teams require location-audited records for regulatory inspections.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Clinics and patient addresses become geofences.
- Reps or nurses scan on arrival to confirm the visit.
- Managers get verified visit reports for incentives and compliance.

### Key features used
- AUDIT/ATTENDANCE, route compliance, visit-order enforcement, privacy-aware records.

### Revenue model
- Per-field-worker license.
- Enterprise contract with compliance reporting.

### Example
A pharmaceutical firm verifies that reps visited 200 clinics in a month. Bonuses are tied to verified visits rather than self-reported numbers.

**Additional examples:**
- A vaccine distributor verifies that cold-chain nurses reached remote clinics.
- A medical-device company pays bonuses only after reps verify visits to hospital departments.
- A home-health provider documents that a nurse attended a post-operative patient at the stated address.
- A clinical research organization tracks patient attendance at trial sites to maintain protocol compliance.
