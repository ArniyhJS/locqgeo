# Retail Loyalty & Foot-Traffic

### Problem
Retailers struggle to convert digital attention into store visits. Traditional loyalty cards are slow, and online clicks do not prove footfall.

**Additional challenges:**
- Online-to-offline attribution is weak; retailers do not know which digital ads actually brought shoppers in.
- Paper loyalty cards are lost, forged, or never redeemed.
- Mall operators lack data on which zones, stores, or events drive the most visits.
- Competitors can set up fake check-ins to manipulate loyalty leaderboards.
- Promotions often attract bargain hunters who never return.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Stores or malls become geofences.
- Shoppers scan at entry or at a product zone to earn points, stamps, or instant rewards.
- Retailers see real visit data tied to campaigns.

### Key features used
- Multi-visit campaigns, streak logic, treasure-hunt ordering, per-location visit weights, wallet integration.

### Revenue model
- Monthly SaaS per store/chain.
- Percentage of reward value.
- Premium analytics reports.

### Example
A mall chain launches “Shop 5 stores, win a shopping voucher.” Each store has a geofence. The app tracks verified visits and issues the voucher automatically.

**Additional examples:**
- A supermarket chain offers a digital stamp card: verify at three store branches to unlock a family voucher.
- A fashion retailer runs a weekend flash campaign where the first 100 verified shoppers receive a discount.
- A mall hosts a food festival and uses geofences to award points for visiting multiple restaurant stalls.
- A beauty brand rewards customers for verifying at branded in-store experience zones.
