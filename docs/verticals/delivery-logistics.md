# Delivery & Logistics

### Problem

Delivery fraud includes fake drop-off photos, drivers marking orders as delivered while still blocks away, and route deviations.

**Additional challenges:**

- Drivers sometimes mark packages as delivered before reaching the address.
- Fake delivery photos create disputes between customers, drivers, and platforms.
- High-value shipments require proof of handover at a precise location.
- Route deviations increase fuel costs and delay SLA compliance.
- Failed delivery attempts are often disputed, leading to refund leakage.

### How SpottGeo helps

- Delivery addresses become geofences.
- Driver scan + GPS verification proves the package reached the destination.
- Customers receive verified delivery confirmation.

### Key features used

- Address geocoding, radius checks, device fingerprint, timestamp verification.

### Revenue model

- Per-delivery verification fee.
- Fleet management SaaS.

### Example

A last-mile courier adds QR stickers to deliveries. The driver scans at the customer’s door; the engine confirms the GPS matches the delivery address before marking complete.

**Additional examples:**

- A pharmacy delivery service verifies that temperature-sensitive medicine reached the patient before leaving.
- An e-commerce platform requires a QR scan at the customer’s door for same-day delivery completion.
- A logistics company tracks whether drivers entered prohibited zones or skipped scheduled stops.
- A food delivery app uses geofence verification to release rider incentives only for on-time drop-offs.
