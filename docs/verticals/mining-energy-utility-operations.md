# Mining, Energy & Utility Operations

### Problem

Energy producers, mining operators, and public utilities manage vast, hazardous, and geographically isolated assets where unverified maintenance or skipped safety inspections cause catastrophic downtime and safety violations.

**Additional challenges:**

- Field technicians claim inspection visits to remote solar arrays, wind turbines, or oil wells that were skipped.
- High risk of safety violations when mandatory Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) walkthroughs are forged.
- Lack of real-time visibility into patrol coverage along high-voltage transmission lines or pipelines.
- Paper audit logs fail to satisfy regulatory compliance bodies during safety incident investigations.
- Remote locations frequently lack continuous cellular coverage.

### How SpottGeo helps

- Wellheads, transformer substations, solar strings, and mining pits are geofenced as critical asset nodes.
- Maintenance crews log offline-capable verification pings upon physical arrival at remote machinery.
- SpottEngine validates velocity, location integrity, and hardware signatures to prevent falsified reports.

### Key features used

- Offline verification queue, anti-cheat velocity checks, device fingerprinting, time-bounded campaigns.

### Revenue model

- Per-asset monthly monitoring fee.
- Enterprise utility contract licensing.

### Example

An oil & gas pipeline operator verifies that line-patrol officers physically walk assigned pipeline segments every 24 hours, automatically updating compliance dashboards.

**Additional examples:**

- A solar farm operator verifies weekly inverter maintenance visits across 500-acre solar arrays.
- A mining company logs verified safety inspector presence at underground shaft access points.
- An electricity distribution company verifies line-worker presence before authorizing high-voltage grid shutoffs.
- A wind turbine manufacturer tracks technician physical presence during warranty service calls.
