# Insurance Claims & Risk Verification

### Problem
Insurers need to verify that inspectors, adjusters, or policyholders actually visited a property or accident scene.

**Additional challenges:**
- Fraudulent claims often rely on staged or misdated photos.
- Adjusters cannot always visit remote locations quickly, delaying settlements.
- Policyholders dispute whether an assessor actually inspected the damage.
- Catastrophe-response teams need to coordinate thousands of site visits across wide regions.
- Claims leakage from inaccurate location data erodes profitability.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Claim locations become geofences.
- Adjusters scan at the site; the engine records verified presence with timestamp and GPS accuracy.
- Reduces fraudulent claims and speeds up processing.

### Key features used
- AUDIT action type, photo/metadata coordination, tamper-proof timestamps.

### Revenue model
- Per-inspection fee.
- Enterprise API for underwriting workflows.

### Example
After a car accident, an insurance adjuster scans a QR at the crash scene. The insurer receives a verified attendance report with exact location and time.

**Additional examples:**
- A motor insurer requires a verifiable geotagged scan at the accident scene before approving repairs.
- A home insurer sends a contractor link; the contractor must verify presence at the property before work begins.
- An agricultural insurer confirms that a loss assessor visited the specific farm plot affected by flooding.
- A travel insurer uses airport geofences to validate trip commencement before processing claims.
