# Waste Management & Sanitation Auditing

### Problem

Municipal governments and waste management agencies pay private contractors to collect refuse and clean drains, but lack automated systems to verify route completion.

**Additional challenges:**

- Contractors skip assigned street sectors while billing for full municipal coverage.
- Dump trucks bypass official waste transfer stations and illegally dump refuse elsewhere.
- Citizens suffer from uncollected waste despite government funds being disbursed.
- Manual inspection teams cannot monitor hundreds of collection points daily across major cities.

### How SpottGeo helps

- Waste collection points, transfer stations, and landfill sites are set up as geofences.
- Truck operators or street sanitation supervisors scan location nodes upon completing collections.
- SpottEngine validates that collection pings occurred within designated operating hours and location bounds.

### Key features used

- Geofence route verification, timestamp logging, automated payout release for verified contractors.

### Revenue model

- SaaS subscription for government municipal waste management boards.
- Per-contractor verification fee.

### Example

A state waste management authority requires private refuse collection trucks to check in at designated residential collection hubs before weekly contract payments are released.

**Additional examples:**

- A city council tracks street-sweeping crews checking in across municipal zones.
- An environmental agency verifies that hazardous medical waste is delivered to approved incineration plants.
- A recycling startup rewards residents with airtime for bringing sorted recyclables to verified collection hubs.
