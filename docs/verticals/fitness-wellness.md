# Fitness & Wellness

### Problem

Gyms and wellness apps struggle to prove members actually visited facilities or completed outdoor challenges.

**Additional challenges:**

- Insurance-linked wellness programs need verified activity, not just app self-reporting.
- Gyms lose members because attendance tracking feels impersonal.
- Outdoor challenges rely on honor systems that can be gamed.
- Corporate wellness budgets are wasted on programs with low participation.
- Personal trainers cannot prove clients attended scheduled outdoor sessions.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Gyms, parks, and route checkpoints become geofences.
- Members scan or auto-check in at locations to earn points or streaks.
- Insurers can offer verified-activity discounts.

### Key features used

- Streak counting, multi-visit campaigns, route checkpoints.

### Revenue model

- Per-member license.
- Health-insurer partnership fees.

### Example

A fitness app partners with gyms: members who verify 12 visits per month get a premium discount from an insurance provider.

**Additional examples:**

- A gym chain rewards members with points for every verified visit, redeemable for merchandise.
- An insurer gives premium discounts after policyholders verify a target number of monthly workouts.
- A running club validates route completion by scanning QR codes at checkpoints.
- A corporate wellness program ranks branches by verified participation in walking challenges.
