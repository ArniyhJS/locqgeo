# Humanitarian Aid & Relief Distribution

### Problem

Non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and international aid agencies face significant leakage, phantom beneficiaries, and verification challenges during disaster response and relief disbursements.

**Additional challenges:**

- Financial aid or food rations are claimed by unauthorized third parties or ghost beneficiaries.
- Field teams operate in low-connectivity zones where real-time database queries fail.
- Donors demand transparent, audit-ready proof of physical distribution to vulnerable populations.
- Ghost workers claim field outreach hours during vaccination or crisis response campaigns.
- Manual paper logs lead to errors, delays, and vulnerability to local corruption.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Relief centers, refugee camps, and mobile clinics are designated as verified distribution hubs.
- Beneficiaries and field workers utilize offline-capable verification (LocqVerify) to confirm physical presence.
- LocqEngine's payout microservice triggers instant, verified cash disbursements or digital vouchers upon presence confirmation.

### Key features used

- Offline verification queue, instant automated payouts, anti-cheat device fingerprinting, velocity checks.

### Revenue model

- Per-verification transaction fee for grant disbursements.
- NGO enterprise platform licensing.

### Example

A disaster relief NGO deploys mobile food distribution trucks in flood-impacted regions, verifying beneficiary presence at GPS coordinates before unlocking digital food vouchers.

**Additional examples:**

- A global health agency tracks community health workers conducting door-to-door polio vaccination visits in rural districts.
- A refugee response agency verifies physical attendance at vocational training centers before releasing stipend payouts.
- An emergency shelter network logs verified nightly occupancy across emergency housing centers.
- A water sanitation initiative verifies field technicians performing well pump maintenance in drought-affected areas.
