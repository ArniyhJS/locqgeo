# Tourism & Hospitality

### Problem

Tourism boards and hotels want to drive exploration and engagement but lack location-based incentives that cannot be gamed.

**Additional challenges:**

- Tourism boards spend on campaigns but cannot measure actual attraction visits.
- Hotel loyalty programs lack location-based redemption options.
- Guided tours depend on manual roll calls.
- Tourists lose paper maps and printed vouchers.
- Attractions struggle to engage repeat visitors.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Landmarks, hotels, restaurants, and tour stops become geofences.
- Tourists scan to collect digital stamps, points, or local rewards.
- Operators see which attractions drive the most engagement.

### Key features used

- Treasure-hunt ordering, badges, wallet integration, leaderboard.

### Revenue model

- Destination marketing fee.
- Hotel/attraction SaaS subscription.
- Reward margin.

### Example

A city runs a “Explore 10 Landmarks” challenge. Visitors earn a city-branded digital badge and discount vouchers at verified locations.

**Additional examples:**

- A city tourism board runs a passport challenge where visitors collect digital stamps at museums.
- A hotel rewards verified check-ins with late checkout or dining credits.
- A walking-tour app unlocks audio narration only after verifying the user reached each stop.
- A nature reserve verifies hikers completed an eco-trail before issuing a conservation certificate.
