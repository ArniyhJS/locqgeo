# Telecom Infrastructure & Fiber Maintenance

### Problem
Telecommunications infrastructure providers spend millions maintaining cell towers and fiber routes, but field engineers often submit fake routine check-in logs.

**Additional challenges:**
- Remote cell towers (BTS sites) are vulnerable to diesel theft, vandalism, and equipment failure.
- Contractors invoice for routine maintenance visits that were never performed.
- Manual paper logs or photo submissions at tower gates are easily falsified.
- Unscheduled downtime increases when preventative maintenance routines are skipped.
- Underground fiber line inspectors fake patrol routes along highway corridors.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Tower compound gates and fiber junction boxes are provisioned as geofenced location nodes.
- Engineers scan location nodes using SpottVerify upon arriving at a site.
- SpottEngine's velocity filter prevents engineers from logging multiple distant tower visits simultaneously.

### Key features used
- Anti-cheat velocity & teleportation filter, geofence radius enforcement, SpottConsole operational telemetry.

### Revenue model
- Per-tower annual monitoring fee for TowerCos (e.g., IHS, American Tower).
- Contractor management SaaS subscription.

### Example
A tower infrastructure company uses SpottEngine to confirm that maintenance crews physically spent 30 minutes at a remote base station before approving service vendor invoices.

**Additional examples:**
- A fiber network operator tracks line-patrol guards walking along optical fiber cable routes.
- A mobile network operator verifies emergency diesel generator refuel visits at off-grid tower sites.
- A satellite ISP confirms technician visits for enterprise VSAT dish installations.
