# Workforce & Field Operations

### Problem

Remote staff often need to prove they were at a site. Manual sign-in sheets are easy to fake, and managers lack real-time visibility.

**Additional challenges:**

- Remote workers can claim site visits that never happened, inflating payroll and service costs.
- Manual sign-in sheets are delayed, incomplete, and easy to falsify.
- Managers lack real-time visibility into field-team coverage and missed locations.
- Compliance audits require paper trails that are hard to reconstruct.
- Route optimization and actual route execution often diverge.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Work sites become geofences.
- Field workers scan on arrival and departure.
- Employers get tamper-proof timesheets, route compliance, and audit trails.

### Key features used

- ATTENDANCE action type, time-bound campaigns, velocity checks, duplicate prevention, device fingerprint.

### Revenue model

- Per-seat monthly subscription.
- Enterprise API contracts.

### Example

A solar installation company verifies that technicians visited each installation site. Payroll and compliance reports pull directly from verification records.

**Additional examples:**

- A telco verifies that maintenance crews visited every base station on their weekly route.
- A power utility confirms inspectors checked transformers across rural districts.
- A cleaning services company uses verified presence to invoice clients based on actual site attendance.
- A road-safety agency confirms wardens were deployed at assigned intersections during peak hours.
