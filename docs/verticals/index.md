# Vertical Use Cases Overview

SpottGeo is a generic Proof-of-Presence platform. A single engine can verify that a person was physically at a location, while the engagement layer turns that verification into business value. This guide maps how different industries can use the system.

---

## Executive Summary

Every vertical below shares the same core need:

> **Prove a real person was at a real place at a real time, without relying on trust or manual paperwork.**

Once that proof exists, it can be used for:

- Fraud reduction
- Marketing attribution
- Automated rewards and incentives
- Compliance and audit trails
- Workforce accountability
- Gamified consumer engagement
