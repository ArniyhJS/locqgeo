# Construction & Infrastructure Management

### Problem

General contractors and infrastructure developers struggle to verify project milestones, site attendance, and equipment deployment across remote construction sites.

**Additional challenges:**

- Subcontractors claim milestone completions for uninspected or incomplete site work.
- Proxy check-ins inflate labor hours and payroll costs on high-budget build sites.
- Heavy machinery is unauthorizedly moved or operated off-site without supervisor approval.
- High costs and delays associated with sending senior site engineers for routine physical checks.
- Audit trails for municipal building compliance lack tamper-proof location data.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Build sites and specific structural zones (foundations, sectors) are converted into active geofences.
- Site engineers and safety inspectors scan upon arrival to log tamper-proof proof-of-presence before signing off on milestones.
- LocqEngine's anti-cheat engine prevents GPS spoofing and proxy check-ins for labor teams.

### Key features used

- ATTENDANCE action type, velocity checks, device fingerprinting, geofencing, custom metadata logs.

### Revenue model

- Per-site monthly subscription.
- Enterprise API contracts for ERP and project management platform integrations.

### Example

An infrastructure firm building a 50km highway requires site engineers to log verified presence at each 5km sector before disbursing subcontractor progress payouts.

**Additional examples:**

- A commercial real estate developer verifies daily safety walkthroughs by site safety officers.
- A municipal public works agency confirms road resurfacing crews operated at assigned road segments.
- A equipment rental company tracks verified check-ins for heavy machinery operators at designated job sites.
- A home builder verifies subcontractor trade completion (plumbing, electrical) before initiating escrow release.
