# Government & Civic Programs

### Problem
Government and NGOs run field programs where attendance or distribution needs to be verified to prevent leakage and ghost beneficiaries.

**Additional challenges:**
- Ghost beneficiaries drain social intervention budgets.
- Field officers cannot quickly prove they reached remote communities.
- Training and distribution programs lack auditable attendance records.
- Election and civic engagement initiatives struggle to verify turnout.
- Donors demand transparent proof of program reach.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Distribution points, training venues, and project sites become geofences.
- Beneficiaries or field officers scan to verify presence.
- Public dashboards show verified participation.

### Key features used
- ATTENDANCE/AUDIT, bulk campaign creation, budget capping, public analytics.

### Revenue model
- Government contract / project-based licensing.
- Audit reporting fees.

### Example
An agricultural extension program verifies that farmers attended training sessions before receiving subsidized inputs.

**Additional examples:**
- A ministry verifies that farmers attended training before receiving subsidized seeds.
- An NGO confirms community health workers visited immunization posts on schedule.
- A local government tracks verified attendance at town-hall meetings to allocate project funds.
- An electoral body verifies that polling-station officials arrived before ballot distribution.
