# Automotive & Mobility

### Problem

Car rental, test-drive, and ride-sharing companies need verified pickup/drop-off locations and route compliance.

**Additional challenges:**

- Car-sharing users report vehicles missing even when the previous renter marked it returned.
- Test-drive promotions attract no-shows or fake location claims.
- Fleet operators cannot prove drivers started and ended routes at depots.
- Rental disputes over fuel and damage often lack verifiable handover timestamps.
- EV charging networks need proof of charger use for subsidy claims.

### How SpottGeo helps

- Rental lots, test-drive zones, and drop-off points become geofences.
- Driver or renter scans at handover to confirm location.
- Reduces disputes over vehicle condition and location.

### Key features used

- AUDIT action, timestamp and GPS verification, device binding.

### Revenue model

- Per-rental/test-drive fee.
- Fleet-management SaaS.

### Example

A car-sharing platform requires a QR scan at the parking bay before unlocking the vehicle, proving the car is actually there.

**Additional examples:**

- A car-sharing app unlocks the next booking only after the previous driver verifies return to the bay.
- A dealership rewards verified test-drive completion with a service voucher.
- A logistics fleet tracks whether trucks departed and returned to the distribution center on schedule.
- An EV charging provider uses geofence verification to confirm subsidy-eligible charging sessions.
