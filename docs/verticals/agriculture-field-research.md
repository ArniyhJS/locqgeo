# Agriculture & Field Research

### Problem
Agritech companies and research institutions need to verify that extension workers or researchers visited specific farms or plots.

**Additional challenges:**
- Agronomists cannot prove they visited specific farms for extension services.
- Research datasets are unreliable when field-visit locations are self-reported.
- Input distribution programs suffer from leakage and favoritism.
- Crop-insurance claims lack independent verification of farm visits.
- Cooperatives struggle to track extension coverage across scattered smallholder plots.

### How SpottGeo helps
- Farm plots and research sites become geofences.
- Field officers scan at each location.
- Data is tied to GPS coordinates and timestamps for audit and analysis.

### Key features used
- AUDIT/ATTENDANCE, offline sync for rural areas, route compliance.

### Revenue model
- Per-field-officer license.
- Research-grant compliance reporting.

### Example
An agritech startup verifies that extension agents visited 500 farms each month to deliver training and collect crop data.

**Additional examples:**
- An agritech platform verifies that field officers trained farmers on new planting techniques.
- A crop-insurance adjuster records verified visits to damaged plots for claim validation.
- A seed company confirms distributors reached assigned agro-dealer locations.
- A research university maps verified soil-sample collection points for climate studies.
