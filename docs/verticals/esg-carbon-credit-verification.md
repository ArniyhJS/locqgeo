# ESG & Carbon Credit Verification (MRV)

### Problem

Environmental funds, carbon credit buyers, and ESG auditors struggle to verify Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) claims for remote green projects.

**Additional challenges:**

- Reforestation and solar installation claims are frequently forged or exaggerated.
- Remote locations lack reliable internet infrastructure to transmit real-time data.
- Carbon credit buyers face reputational damage from "greenwashing" scandals.
- Field auditors charge high fees for manual physical site inspections.
- Lack of cryptographic and GPS-backed audit trails for environmental grants.

### How LocqGeo helps

- Plantation sites and renewable installations are designated as geofenced nodes.
- Field workers use LocqVerify to record offline-capable, GPS-verified tree planting or maintenance pings.
- LocqEngine's anti-cheat pipeline prevents spoofed coordinates from claiming credits.

### Key features used

- Offline verification queue, anti-cheat velocity/teleportation checks, immutable audit logging.

### Revenue model

- Per-verification transaction fee for carbon registries.
- Enterprise SaaS for ESG fund management.

### Example

A global carbon fund requires tree-planting teams in rural Nigeria to verify presence at planting coordinates before issuing micro-payouts for each verified hectare.

**Additional examples:**

- A solar energy initiative verifies site visits by maintenance crews before disbursing clean-energy subsidies.
- A mangrove restoration NGO tracks field workers planting seedlings along remote coastal regions.
- An agricultural ESG project verifies sustainable farming practice adoption across registered cooperative farms.
